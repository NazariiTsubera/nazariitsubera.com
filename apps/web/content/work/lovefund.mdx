## What it is

LoveFund is a donation platform at Global Virtual Opportunities. The thing that makes it different is a decision about trust: every campaign is reviewed by a person before it is published. The discover page says so in its first line, and means it.

<Figure src="lovefund-2.png" />

## Built around the review

Most fundraising platforms are self-service and fight fraud after the fact. LoveFund puts the review first. A campaign is drafted, submitted and checked, and only then does it appear on the discover page with its story, its goal and how far along it is. Trust is part of the workflow rather than something bolted on when a problem appears.

It also keeps the catalogue small on purpose. A donor sees a handful of causes that someone stands behind, not an endless feed to scroll past.

## Giving in one screen

<Figure src="lovefund-3.png" />

The checkout is a single page. Preset amounts and a custom field, a summary that shows the total before a single personal detail is asked for, and the fee model stated in plain words: organisers pay no platform fee, only the card processor’s standard charge applies to a gift, and the platform runs on an optional tip that the donor sets with a slider. Nothing is hidden in a later step, which is the difference between a donor who finishes and one who closes the tab.
