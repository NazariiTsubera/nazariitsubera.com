## What it is

LoveFund is a donation platform at Global Virtual Opportunities. The thing that makes it different is a decision about trust: every campaign is reviewed by a person before it is published.

## Building around the review

Most fundraising platforms are self-service, and they fight fraud after the fact. LoveFund puts the review step first, so the platform is built around it: a campaign is drafted, submitted, checked, and only then goes live. Trust is part of the workflow rather than something bolted on when a problem appears.

I build and run the platform alongside MarkoMax, on the same infrastructure described in the other articles here.
