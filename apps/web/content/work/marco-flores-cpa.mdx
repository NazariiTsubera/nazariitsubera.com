## What Marco needed

Two things. To be found by people looking for an accountant in San Antonio, and a safe way for his clients to hand over documents without an email thread or a trip to the office.

<Figure src="marco-flores-cpa-2.png" />

## What I built

A website that does the first, and a secure client portal that does the second. Client files sit behind a login instead of in an inbox, and a client who wants to send something can do it without picking up the phone.

<Figure src="marco-flores-cpa-3.png" />
