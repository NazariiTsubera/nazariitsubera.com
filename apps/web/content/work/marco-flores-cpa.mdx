## What Marco needed

Two things. To be found by the people looking for an accountant in San Antonio, and a safe way for clients to hand over documents without an email thread or a trip to the office.

## The site

<Figure src="marco-flores-cpa-2.png" />

The site is written for the people who hire a CPA, not for other accountants. The home page says who it is for and puts the credentials where a first-time client looks for them: licensed Texas CPA, member of the Texas Society of CPAs, in practice since 2014. Each service has its own page in plain language, so a small-business owner searching for help with a Schedule C lands on a page about exactly that rather than on a generic list.

<Figure src="marco-flores-cpa-3.png" />

The reviews page reproduces the firm’s Google reviews in full and says so. Nothing is reworded, and the rating and the count sit next to them. For a practice built on trust, that is worth more than any line of copy I could write.

## The portal

Behind the “Client Portal” link is a secure area where clients sign in and hand over documents. Files sit behind a login instead of in an inbox, and a client who wants to send something at nine in the evening can do it without waiting for office hours or picking up the phone.
